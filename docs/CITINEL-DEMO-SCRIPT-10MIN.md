# CITINEL
## The ten-minute demo, baby steps

*Two people. One clicks. One speaks. Every **BOLD CAPITAL** word is printed on
the screen. Every SAY line is short and natural. Do the steps in order. The time
on the left keeps you on ten minutes.*

---

# How to use this page

- Clicker reads the numbered lines. One action each.
- Speaker reads only the **SAY** lines.
- **[pause]** means one full second of silence.
- The speaker starts each SAY as the click lands.
- Never read ahead. Trust the order.

---

# Part A · Before the judges arrive

## Fifteen minutes before

1. Run `python3 scripts/preflight.py` in a terminal. It must print **GO**.
2. Open Render. Click **citinel-web**. Click **Manual Deploy**. Once.
3. Wait 90 seconds.
4. Open the app. Click **ANALYST** at the top left.
5. Click **CONNECTORS**. Find **ARM THIS DEVICE**.
6. Type your name. Paste the token. Click **SAVE**.
7. Check the green dot and **ARMED** at the foot of the rail.
8. Click **REPLAY**. Open the **INCIDENT** dropdown. Choose **INC-0417**.
9. The chip must read **STATE CAUGHT**. If CLOSED, click **REOPEN FOR RE-INVESTIGATION**, OK, OK.
10. Open the dropdown again. Choose **INC-0419**. Same check. Same fix.
11. Scroll the right column to **THREAT BRIEF · TAVILY RESEARCH**.
12. Click **REQUEST THREAT BRIEF**. Wait for **ASK AGAIN**.
13. Click **COMPLIANCE**. Wait 20 seconds. Say nothing. This warms it.
14. Click **REPLAY**. Choose **INC-0417** again. Leave it here.
15. Close every other tab. Full screen. Hide bookmarks.

## The four rules for the speaker

- 🔴 Never say a prize is certain.
- 🔴 Never say a false-positive number. It is unmeasured.
- 🔴 Never say an action touched a real system. All simulated.
- 🔴 Never say Slack works unless it worked on screen today.

# If something goes wrong

- Red banner **AUTHORED DEMO** on Compliance. SAY: "Our own guard. Live record did not load." Press Cmd+R. Wait.
- Witness reads **unavailable**. SAY: "Third party not answering. We report it. Not assume it."
- Receipt reads **policy_blocked**. SAY: "A blocked action is the guardrail working." Best moment possible.
- **APPROVE AND FIRE** is grey. Click the key dial again.
- Compliance is slow. Say nothing for twenty seconds. It is warm.
- Anything else. Click **AUDIT**. SAY: "Whatever happened, the ledger recorded it."

# If you are short on time

Drop these, in this order. Nothing else depends on them.

1. HANDOVER and EXEC. Saves 30 seconds.
2. CORPUS. Saves 20 seconds.
3. DEMO. Saves 35 seconds.
4. CONFIDENCE. Saves 25 seconds.

Never drop REPLAY, APPROVALS, COMPLIANCE, AUDIT or EVAL.
