# CITINEL — RATIO-TRUE PLACEMENT SKELETONS
The outer rectangle IS the 1920x1080 slide, drawn to true 16:9. **Every inner box has its image's real shape and real size** — wide box = wide image, square = square, tall = tall. Place each PNG where and how big its box sits; the shape does the thinking.
Scale: 1 character = 20 px across, 1 line = 40 px down. `A) B) C) D)` = text zones — full wording in CITINEL_Master_Assembly_Guide.md Part 2. `2:1 crop` = trim that rail's white top/bottom first. Top and bottom bands: never touch.
